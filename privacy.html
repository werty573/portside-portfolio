Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

Portside Digital builds websites for businesses in Trinidad & Tobago. This page explains what we collect when you contact us or fill in one of our forms, including forms on Facebook and Instagram, and what we do with it.

What we collect

  • Your name and phone or WhatsApp number
  • Your email address, if you give it
  • What you tell us about your business and the website you want, such as what you sell, your budget and when you want to start
  • Messages you send us on WhatsApp, Facebook or Instagram

How we use it

  • To reply to you and send you a quote
  • To plan, build and support your website if you hire us
  • To follow up about your enquiry. We won't add you to a mailing list without asking.

Who we share it with

We do not sell or rent your information. We only share it with the services we use to run the business, such as Meta (Facebook, Instagram and WhatsApp), and only as needed to talk to you and deliver your project. We may also share it if the law requires us to.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry details for up to 12 months. For clients, we keep project records for as long as we support your website, plus any period the law requires.

Your choices

You can ask us at any time to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you, or to stop contacting you. Just message us on WhatsApp.
